How to use the reading set

Read in playlist order. Follow a timestamp when you want the instructor's context, then work through the independent derivation. Attempt exercises before opening their solutions. Use the interactive numerical lab to test how sampling, verification, and reliability depend on assumptions.

Prerequisites: undergraduate probability, basic calculus and linear algebra, and familiarity with language models. Each chapter defines its own specialized notation. For system-building questions, connect the chapters on tools, planning, learning, and evaluation.
